# InfraGuard AI

> Predictive Maintenance Platform for Public Infrastructure

InfraGuard AI is an AI-powered predictive maintenance platform designed to help public authorities identify infrastructure that may require maintenance before failures become critical.

The platform combines citizen complaints, infrastructure asset data, inspection information, historical maintenance patterns, environmental conditions, and machine-learning-based risk prediction to help maintenance teams prioritize their work.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Public infrastructure such as roads, bridges, drainage systems, water pipelines, streetlights, and public buildings requires continuous monitoring and maintenance.

Traditional maintenance workflows often depend on manual inspections and reactive complaints.

InfraGuard AI aims to make this process more proactive by providing:

- Citizen-based issue reporting
- Infrastructure asset management
- AI-based maintenance risk prediction
- Complaint prioritization
- Officer verification workflows
- Field inspection and verification
- Maintenance work-order tracking
- Infrastructure analytics
- Role-based dashboards

The goal is to help authorities identify high-risk infrastructure earlier and allocate maintenance resources more effectively.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Public infrastructure can deteriorate gradually due to:

- Age
- Repeated failures
- Environmental conditions
- High usage
- Poor physical condition
- Increasing citizen complaints
- Delayed inspections
- Lack of centralized maintenance information

Without a unified system, maintenance teams may struggle to determine which assets require immediate attention.

InfraGuard AI addresses this problem by bringing infrastructure information, citizen reports, AI predictions, prioritization, and field verification into one platform.
