ABOUT INFRAGUARD AI

A digital platform for responsive public infrastructure

Public infrastructure requires continuous inspection, timely maintenance and effective citizen participation. InfraGuard AI brings these activities together in a single digital workflow.

Historical maintenance information, citizen complaints, inspections, asset condition and environmental factors can help identify infrastructure that may require attention.

  • Citizen-centric complaint reporting with location and photo evidence.
  • GIS-based identification of nearby public infrastructure assets.
  • AI-assisted risk prediction and maintenance prioritization.
  • Transparent status tracking from submission to resolution.

HOW IT WORKS

From citizen report to verified resolution

Click each stage to explore the service workflow.

Citizen reports an issue

A citizen submits an infrastructure complaint with a description, severity, location and optional photo evidence.

Nearby asset is identified

Geographic coordinates help the platform identify the infrastructure asset associated with the complaint.

AI assesses maintenance risk

Asset condition, complaint history, failures, maintenance intervals, inspection scores and environmental factors contribute to risk assessment.

Officers prioritize and assign

Maintenance officers review the recommendation, prioritize the issue and coordinate the required maintenance action.

Field verification and closure

Field inspectors verify the work and citizens can confirm the outcome through the service workflow.
